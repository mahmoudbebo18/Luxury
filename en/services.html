01

Legal consultations

Experienced lawyers and legal advisors for your private matters, and follow-up of your court transactions through Najiz.

What's included

  • Private, confidential consultations
  • Selected services through Najiz
  • Legal guidance and document review

How to request it

In your account, choose "Legal consultations" and an email opens with your member number filled in. Explain the matter and attach any documents; the lawyer replies to your own address.

Your consultation stays between you and the lawyer. If your membership comes through your company, the company never sees the services you request. The number of consultations included each year is in the terms & conditions.

02

Roadside assistance

Car broke down? Send your location and help is on its way, in the city or on the highway.

What's included

  • Towing with the right flatbed for your vehicle
  • Tyres: fixed on the spot or taken to a specialist
  • Battery charge or replacement
  • Petrol or diesel delivery

How to request it

Tap the WhatsApp button in your account to send a help request with your member number and current location. Add the problem, your car and its colour, and we send the nearest help.

The number of requests included each year, the coverage area and what towing includes are set out in the terms & conditions.

03

Travel visas

We take your tourist visa application from start to finish with no extra service fee. You pay embassy fees only.

What's included

  • Application and required documents prepared
  • Embassy or visa-centre appointment booked
  • Followed up until the visa is issued

How to request it

Message us on WhatsApp from your account with the country, travel date and number of travellers. We send you the list of documents and take care of the rest.

Government and embassy fees are paid by the member, and the decision to grant a visa rests with the embassy alone. The number of applications included each year is in the terms & conditions.

How requests work

Every service, from your account.

  1. Pick a service

    Under "My services" in your account, with what's left of each service in your membership year.

  2. Send the request

    A WhatsApp message or email opens with your name and member number, so there's nothing to explain.

  3. We see it through

    We coordinate with the provider, who checks your membership with your digital card and QR code.
