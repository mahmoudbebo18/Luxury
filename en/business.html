Business FAQ

Before you register.

Is the company administrator a member automatically?
No. The administrator account is for managing memberships. If you want a card yourself, invite your own email to a seat like any employee.
What if we need more memberships during the year?
Add them from the company dashboard at any time. They end with the rest of your memberships, so you only pay for the remaining months.
Can the administrator see which services an employee uses?
No. The administrator sees the employee's name and membership status only. The services an employee requests, legal consultations above all, stay between them and the club.
If a membership moves to another employee, do the service limits reset?
No. Service limits belong to the membership for its whole year, as set out in the terms & conditions.
