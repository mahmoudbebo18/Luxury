FAQ

Before you join.

What does the membership include?
Three services: legal consultations, roadside assistance and travel visas, under the terms & conditions and the limits set out for each service.
How long is the membership, and when does it start?
A full year from the moment you pay. We remind you before it ends, and when you renew the new year is added to your current end date, so you never lose a day.
How do I request a service?
Pick the service in your account and a WhatsApp message opens with your name and member number filled in. For roadside assistance you can share your location directly.
How does business membership work?
Your company registers and chooses how many memberships it needs, then invites employees from its dashboard. Each employee gets their own account and a card with the company's name. All of a company's memberships end on the same date and renew on one invoice.
How can I pay?
mada, Visa, Mastercard and Apple Pay through a secure payment gateway. Companies can also pay by bank transfer.
